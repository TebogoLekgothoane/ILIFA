Build a polished mobile-first application called PASTPORT.

TAGLINE:
“Places remember. Stories live again.”

CORE CONCEPT:
PASTPORT is an immersive heritage tourism platform that turns real-world historical locations into interactive “time machines.”

A visitor can discover a historical location, physically go there, point their phone camera at it, and use an AR-style experience to see how the location looked in the past.

The app combines:
- GPS/location
- camera-based AR experience
- historical photographs
- 3D historical reconstructions
- AI narration
- conversational AI
- historical timelines
- self-guided heritage trails
- multilingual support

IMPORTANT PRODUCT POSITIONING:
Do NOT make this feel like a generic tourism app or ChatGPT wrapper.

The central experience is:

REAL LOCATION
→ SELECT HISTORICAL PERIOD
→ SHOW ME THEN
→ EXPERIENCE THE PAST
→ ASK QUESTIONS
→ EXPLORE

The AI is supporting the experience, not the product itself.
